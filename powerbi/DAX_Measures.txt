STAGE 3 — POWER BI DAX MEASURES
---------------------------------
Create each formula as a SEPARATE measure under Modeling > New measure.
Do not paste this entire text file as one expression.
The table names must be exactly: FactRequests, FactPayments, FactExceptions.

Total Purchase Requests =
COALESCE(DISTINCTCOUNT(FactRequests[request_id]), 0)

Total Payments =
COALESCE(DISTINCTCOUNT(FactPayments[payment_id]), 0)

Total Findings =
COALESCE(DISTINCTCOUNT(FactExceptions[finding_id]), 0)

High Risk Findings =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Findings],
    FactExceptions[severity] = "High"
)

Open Findings =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Findings],
    FactExceptions[status] = "Open"
)

Open High Risk Findings =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Findings],
    FactExceptions[severity] = "High",
    FactExceptions[status] = "Open"
)

Affected Suppliers =
COALESCE(DISTINCTCOUNT(FactExceptions[supplier_id]), 0)

Approved Purchase Requests =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Purchase Requests],
    FactRequests[approval_status] = "Approved"
)

NOTE ABOUT RELATED AMOUNT:
FactExceptions[related_amount_lkr] is an associated transaction value, NOT
confirmed financial loss. An exception can overlap another exception.
Do not label SUM(FactExceptions[related_amount_lkr]) as "Fraud Loss".

EXPECTED BASELINE WITHOUT FILTERS:
Total Purchase Requests = 20
Total Payments = 21
Total Findings = 4
High Risk Findings = 3
Open Findings = 4
Open High Risk Findings = 3
Affected Suppliers = 4

DATE FILTERING:
Dimension dates filter requests by requested_on, payments by paid_on,
and exceptions by the UNDERLYING EVENT DATE (not detection date).
This means date-sliced totals reflect different event types. That is intentional.
